# THUNDER DOME — TikTok Viral Marketing & Acquisition Plan

## Executive Acquisition Strategy

The primary organic user acquisition engine for **Thunder Dome** is short-form vertical video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts). Retro-modern bullet-hell gameplay combined with heavy synthwave/electronic soundtracks creates high visual and acoustic retention.

---

## 5 High-Conversion TikTok Video Concepts

### Concept 1: "The Boss Hologram Interrupts My Radio" (Taunt & Holo-Comms Hook)
- **Hook (0:00–0:02):** Screen shows player jamming to a high-energy 3000 Studios track in cockpit view. Suddenly, the music cuts to heavy static and a red holographic villain pops up taunting: *"You think you own these skies, rookie?"*
- **Body (0:03–0:12):** Player selects the response *"WATCH THIS"* on screen, switches to 3rd-person follow camera, triggers Chrono Stasis slow-mo, and weaves through 150 incoming plasma bolts.
- **Climax (0:13–0:20):** Swarm missiles lock onto the boss hardpoints, detonating the flagship in a massive fiery explosion with screen trauma shake.
- **Audio:** Custom 3000 Studios audio switch (Radio -> Static -> Taunt -> Heavy Drop).
- **CTA:** *"Can you beat Sector 24? Link in bio to play on Android!"*

### Concept 2: "120 FPS Cockpit View vs Top-Down View" (Visual Comparison)
- **Hook (0:00–0:03):** Split-screen comparing 1st-person cockpit holo-HUD with 3rd-person tactical follow and retro 2D top-down mode.
- **Body (0:04–0:15):** Highlighting how changing camera mode transforms the entire game feel, showing laser beams reflecting off the cockpit canopy in Neo Tokyo.
- **Audio:** *Burn Up The Bass* (3000 Studios).
- **CTA:** *"Which camera mode are you choosing? Download Thunder Dome free on Google Play."*

### Concept 3: "I Built a Game to Play My Own Studio Music" (Developer Story Hook)
- **Hook (0:00–0:03):** Text overlay: *"I'm a music producer and got tired of mobile games with trash sound, so I built an entire air combat arcade game around our 27-track album."*
- **Body (0:04–0:18):** Montage showing in-game radio station switching from *Creek Road Lantern* to *Floor Ya* and *Subwoofer From Hell* while dodging bullet curtains.
- **Audio:** 3000 Studios original tracks.
- **CTA:** *"Stream the OST or fly to it in Thunder Dome. Link in bio!"*

### Concept 4: "Near-Death + Ad Revive Clutch in Sector 18" (Gaming Clutch Hook)
- **Hook (0:00–0:03):** Player health hits 0 HP with boss at 5% health. Dramatic slow-mo sound effect.
- **Body (0:04–0:14):** Quick cut showing instant revive + invulnerability shield activation -> unleashing NOVA BURST special ability -> obliterating the boss.
- **CTA:** *"Clutched it with 1 second left. Who can beat my high score of 128,400?"*

### Concept 5: "1v1 Dogfight Against My Best Friend" (PvP Multiplayer Hook)
- **Hook (0:00–0:03):** Side-by-side phones playing real-time 1v1 multiplayer dogfight.
- **Body (0:04–0:15):** Intense maneuvers, barrel rolls dodging swarm missiles, and a point-blank laser kill.
- **Audio:** Energetic battle SFX + hype voiceover commentary.
- **CTA:** *"Tag a friend you can beat in a 1v1 dogfight. Available now!"*
