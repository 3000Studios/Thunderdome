## Full Description (Google Play 4000 Char Limit)

Take the skies in **Thunder Dome**, the ultimate retro-modern arcade air combat shooter built from the ground up for high-performance mobile devices. Pilot hyper-advanced warbirds, upgrade devastating beam primaries and swarm missiles, survive lethal weather anomalies, and conquer 24 handcrafted global combat theaters.

### 🚀 24 HANDCRAFTED COMBAT THEATERS & BOSS ENCOUNTERS
Engage across 24 intense tactical sectors—from the scorched canyons of Rust Canyon to the electrified plasma clouds of Neo Tokyo and the radioactive wastes of Chernobyl. Each stage delivers unique visual atmospheres, dynamic weather hazards (acid rain, ion storms, crosswind turbulence, slowing nanite goo), and colossal multi-phase boss flagships with destructible weapon hardpoints.

### 📡 DYNAMIC HOLOGRAPHIC COMMS & BOSS TAUNTS
Face intelligent enemy commanders who disrupt your radio frequency, project real-time holographic comms into your cockpit, and taunt your piloting skill at critical combat checkpoints. Choose your tactical voice replies to alter battlefield morale and trigger retaliatory combat phases.

### ⚡ 120 FPS FLUIDITY & MULTIPLE CAMERA MODES
Engineered for ultra-smooth 60 to 120 FPS combat with zero stutter. Experience combat through 3 distinct visual perspectives:
- **3rd-Person Tactical Follow:** Full theater situational awareness and precision banking.
- **1st-Person Cockpit View:** Immersive cockpit canopy glass with real-time tactical holo-HUDs.
- **Satellite Top-Down:** Classic retro shoot-'em-up top-down view for bullet-hell perfection.

### ⚔️ REAL-TIME 1v1 MULTIPLAYER DOGFIGHTS
Test your air supremacy against other pilots worldwide in synchronous low-latency 1v1 PvP dogfights. Out-turn, out-juke, and lock onto your opponent with laser-guided swarm salvos.

### 📻 INTEGRATED 3000 STUDIOS SOUNDTRACK RADIO
Switch between over 25 high-octane electronic and synthwave tracks produced by 3000 Studios. Tune the in-game radio directly from your flight HUD or hold down to toggle tactical silent running.

### 🛠️ DEEP HANGAR PROGRESSION & ROGUELITE OVERCLOCKS
- **Warbird Arsenal:** Unlock and upgrade 5 distinct airframes, including the nimble Phoenix Interceptor, heavy Dreadnought Gunship, stealth Ghost Phantom, and the exclusive gold-plated Apex Founder Zero.
- **Modular Weapon Tuning:** Customize primaries (Pulse, Plasma, Laser, Scatter), secondaries (Swarm Missiles, EMP Shockwaves), and specials (Nova Burst, Chrono Stasis Slow-Mo, Ion Shield).
- **Roguelite Overclocks:** Level up mid-sortie to draft system overclocks and craft game-breaking combat synergies.

### 💎 FAIR MONETIZATION & OFFLINE READY
- Play anywhere—singleplayer campaign sorties are 100% playable completely offline.
- No forced unskippable ads during dogfights or boss encounters.
- Optional Founder's Pack and Ad-Free upgrades provide permanent perks and instant continues.
