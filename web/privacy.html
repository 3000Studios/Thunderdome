Privacy

Effective: October 1, 2026

Thunder Dome and this website are operated by 3000 Studios. This page describes the categories of data the service is designed to process. Production deployment must be kept consistent with the actual SDKs, analytics providers, ad products, account systems, and multiplayer services enabled in the released app.

Game and account data

The service may process account identifiers, public display names, gameplay progress, scores, match results, selected settings, anti-abuse signals, and technical event data required to operate leaderboards and multiplayer features.

Advertising and analytics

If advertising or analytics is enabled, those providers may process device, browser, app, interaction, and consent information under their own terms. Google advertising must not be enabled until the production site and app use the required consent, disclosure, and policy configuration for the regions where they are offered.

Public leaderboard

Leaderboard visibility should be opt-in or clearly disclosed. The backend design includes an is-public flag so a player's ranking can be excluded from public display.

Security

Authentication tokens and server credentials are not stored in public source. Score-critical and reward-critical events should be server validated before they are accepted as authoritative.

Deletion and support

Players should be provided a working support channel for account, privacy, and deletion requests before public launch. The production contact method must be added to the Support page and Google Play listing.
