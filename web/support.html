Support

Launch requirement: add the final support email or ticket URL here before publishing this page and before using it in the Google Play listing. No contact address is invented in source.

When reporting a problem

Include the game version, Android version, device model, stage or multiplayer mode, what you expected, what happened, and whether the problem is repeatable. Do not post passwords, authentication tokens, payment credentials, or private account data.

Network diagnostics

The production backend exposes /health. Multiplayer clients should record room ID, connection state, latency, and non-sensitive error codes so support can distinguish device, network, and service failures.

Account and privacy requests

The production support channel should accept account deletion, public leaderboard removal, and privacy requests. Those workflows must be tested before public release.
